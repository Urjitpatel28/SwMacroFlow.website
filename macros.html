Macro library

A library of SOLIDWORKS macros, ready to download.

Every macro here is plain SOLIDWORKS VBA in a .swp file. Each one runs on its own in the VBA editor, and runs across a whole folder unattended inside SwMacroFlow. Open a macro to read its notes and download it.

.swp macro

Change Document Unit

Sets the document's unit system and the individual length, angle, mass, volume, time, force, power and energy units. Optionally switches inch dimensions to fractions.

.swp macro

Change Drawing Template

Applies a .slddrt sheet format to one sheet, several named sheets, or every sheet in a drawing, then rebuilds and saves.

.swp macro

Insert Block In Drawings

Inserts a .sldblk sketch block at a fixed position on one sheet, several named sheets, or every sheet in a drawing, then rebuilds and saves.

.swp macro

Save PDF

Exports the open document to PDF. Works on parts, assemblies and drawings.

.swp macro

Save with Performance Improvement

Turns image quality down to its cheapest settings — shaded deviation at maximum, high-quality edges off, wireframe quality at its lowest — then saves a copy named <name>_Optimized.

Put the downloaded file in this folder, then open SwMacroFlow and choose it from the library.

%LOCALAPPDATA%\SwMacroFlow\macros
