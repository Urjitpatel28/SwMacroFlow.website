Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

SwMacroFlow is a Windows desktop application that runs SOLIDWORKS macros on your own computer, plus a small account system that decides whether your licence is valid. This policy covers both. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

We collect your email address so you can have an account, and a one-way fingerprint of your computer so a licence can only be active on one machine at a time. We do not see your CAD files, your macros, or anything you do inside SOLIDWORKS. We do not sell anything to anyone.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Your email address It identifies your account, confirms sign-up, resets your password, and is how we reach you about your licence.
Your Google or Microsoft identity, if you sign in that way We receive your email address and a user identifier from them so we can recognise you on the next sign-in. We do not receive your password, and we do not ask either provider for access to your files, contacts, or mail.
A one-way fingerprint of your computer A salted SHA-256 hash of the identifier Windows generates when it is installed. It cannot be reversed back into that identifier, and it is used for exactly one thing: knowing whether the computer asking to run SwMacroFlow is the one your licence is on.
Your computer's name (for example OFFICE-PC) Stored as you see it, so that when your licence is in use elsewhere we can say "active on OFFICE-PC" instead of showing you a hash you could not act on.
A record of activations and releases Which computer was activated or released, and when. It is what lets us answer honestly when someone asks why their licence will not move, and it is the only way to notice a licence being shared across an office.
Ordinary server logs Our hosting provider records IP addresses and timestamps for requests, as any web service does, to keep the service running and to limit abuse.

What we do not collect

  • Nothing about your work. No CAD files, no file names, no folder paths, no macro code, no batch results. All of that stays on your computer; the licence check sends only your account token and the fingerprint above.
  • No usage analytics or telemetry. The application does not report what you do with it. The website carries no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no advertising cookies.
  • No payment card details. Payments are not implemented yet. When they are, card details will go to the payment processor and never to us.
  • Your AI Copilot API key never reaches us. If you use the Copilot panel, the key you enter is encrypted on your own machine with Windows data protection and is sent only to the AI provider you chose.

Who processes it

Accounts and licence records are hosted on Supabase, which acts as our data processor and stores the data on its cloud infrastructure. Confirmation and password-reset emails are sent through a third-party email provider. This website is served by GitHub Pages. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, that sign-in happens on their systems under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Your account and licence record last as long as your account does. Activation history is kept while the account exists, because it is what a support conversation about a blocked licence depends on. Server logs are kept for a short period by our host and then discarded.

Your choices

  • See what we hold. Your account page shows your email, your plan, the computer your licence is on, and how many device changes you have used.
  • Release a computer at any time from your account page or from within the application, which clears the fingerprint currently marked active.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction, or deletion by emailing us. Deleting your account removes your email address, your licence, and its activation history, and releases any computer it was on. It cannot be undone.

Children

SwMacroFlow is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, email account holders.
