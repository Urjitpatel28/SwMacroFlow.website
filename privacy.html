Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

SwMacroFlow is a Windows desktop application that runs SOLIDWORKS macros on your own computer. The website offers a free 1 month trial, one-time licence purchases, and accounts for licence management.

The short version

We do not collect your CAD files, macro code, batch results, folders, file names, or SOLIDWORKS activity. The application is designed to do its work locally on your machine.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Ordinary website logs GitHub Pages may process IP addresses, request timestamps, and similar server log data to serve the website and keep it reliable.
Account email and sign-in data Supabase Auth stores the email address and the details needed to create, secure, and manage your account. Your password is stored only as a salted hash, never as text.
Email you send us If you contact support, we use your email address and message content to reply.
A device identifier So a licence activates one computer at a time, the application creates a one-way hash of two Windows values and sends only that hash. We cannot reverse it, and it does not tell us your hardware, your computer name, or who you are. See how the device identifier works below.
Licence and payment records If you buy a licence, we keep the fact that you own one and payment references from Razorpay, so we can provide the licence and issue receipts.

How the device identifier works

The application combines your Windows machine GUID and your system drive's volume serial number with a fixed SwMacroFlow-specific value, then hashes the result. Only the resulting hash leaves your computer. The original values are never transmitted or stored by us.

Because the hash is salted with a value unique to SwMacroFlow, it cannot be used to recognise your computer across any other product or service. We use it for exactly one purpose: enforcing one activation per licence, and making sure a free trial is offered once per computer.

What we do not collect

  • No CAD content. Your models, drawings, assemblies, file names, folder paths, macro code, and batch results stay on your computer.
  • No application telemetry. The app does not report what macros you run or how you use SOLIDWORKS.
  • No website analytics or advertising tracking. This website does not use tracking pixels or advertising cookies.
  • No card or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay. Your card number, UPI ID, and bank credentials are never sent to us and we never see them.
  • No hardware inventory. We do not collect your computer name, serial numbers, MAC address, installed software, or SOLIDWORKS licence details. Only the one-way device hash described above.
  • Your AI Copilot API key never reaches us. Copilot is bring your own key: we do not supply keys, and the key you enter is stored on your own machine and sent only to the AI provider you chose. What you send to that provider is governed by their privacy policy, not ours.

Who processes it

This website is served by GitHub Pages. Account sign-in, licence records, and licence status are handled by Supabase. One-time payments are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy; we hold no payment mandate against your card or bank account. Account and licence email is delivered by our transactional email provider.

How long we keep it

Website logs are retained by the hosting provider according to its own operational policies. Account records are kept while your account is active or as needed for security, legal, and operational reasons. Support emails are kept only as long as they are useful for the support conversation and ordinary record keeping.

Your choices

  • You can browse the site, docs, and macro library without signing in. An account is needed to start the free trial.
  • You can ask us to delete your account or support emails, unless we need to keep them for a legal or security reason.

Children

SwMacroFlow is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we will update the date at the top.

Contact

Email info@swmacroflow.in for anything in this policy.
