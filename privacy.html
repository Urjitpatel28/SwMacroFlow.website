Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

SwMacroFlow is a Windows desktop application that runs SOLIDWORKS macros on your own computer. The website now offers a public download without registration or access checks.

The short version

We do not collect your CAD files, macro code, batch results, folders, file names, or SOLIDWORKS activity. The application is designed to do its work locally on your machine.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Ordinary website logs GitHub Pages may process IP addresses, request timestamps, and similar server log data to serve the website and keep it reliable.
Email you send us If you contact support, we use your email address and message content to reply.
Optional donation details, if added later If a donation flow is added, payment details will be handled by the payment provider. We will not ask you for card details directly on this website.

What we do not collect

  • No CAD content. Your models, drawings, assemblies, file names, folder paths, macro code, and batch results stay on your computer.
  • No application telemetry. The app does not report what macros you run or how you use SOLIDWORKS.
  • No website analytics or advertising tracking. This website does not use tracking pixels or advertising cookies.
  • No registration data. The website no longer provides user-registration or user-management pages.
  • Your AI Copilot API key never reaches us. If you use the Copilot panel, the key you enter is stored on your own machine and sent only to the AI provider you chose.

Who processes it

This website is served by GitHub Pages. Email support may be handled by the mail provider you use to contact us. If optional donations are added later, payment processing will be handled by a payment provider under its own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

Website logs are retained by the hosting provider according to its own operational policies. Support emails are kept only as long as they are useful for the support conversation and ordinary record keeping.

Your choices

  • You can use SwMacroFlow without registering.
  • You can ask us to delete support emails you sent us, unless we need to keep them for a legal or security reason.
  • If a donation flow is added later, donation receipts or payment records will be handled by the payment provider.

Children

SwMacroFlow is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we will update the date at the top.
