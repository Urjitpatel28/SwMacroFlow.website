Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

SwMacroFlow is a Windows desktop application that runs SOLIDWORKS macros on your own computer. The website offers a 6 month trial in this release and supports accounts for account, license, and future billing management.

The short version

We do not collect your CAD files, macro code, batch results, folders, file names, or SOLIDWORKS activity. The application is designed to do its work locally on your machine.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Ordinary website logs GitHub Pages may process IP addresses, request timestamps, and similar server log data to serve the website and keep it reliable.
Account email and sign-in data Supabase Auth stores the email address and sign-in provider details needed to create, secure, and manage your account.
Email you send us If you contact support, we use your email address and message content to reply.

What we do not collect

  • No CAD content. Your models, drawings, assemblies, file names, folder paths, macro code, and batch results stay on your computer.
  • No application telemetry. The app does not report what macros you run or how you use SOLIDWORKS.
  • No website analytics or advertising tracking. This website does not use tracking pixels or advertising cookies.
  • No payment data in this release. Paid plans, subscriptions, and license enforcement are not active yet.
  • Your AI Copilot API key never reaches us. If you use the Copilot panel, the key you enter is stored on your own machine and sent only to the AI provider you chose.

Who processes it

This website is served by GitHub Pages. Account sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth. Google processes sign-in data when you choose Google sign-in. Email support may be handled by the mail provider you use to contact us.

How long we keep it

Website logs are retained by the hosting provider according to its own operational policies. Account records are kept while your account is active or as needed for security, legal, and operational reasons. Support emails are kept only as long as they are useful for the support conversation and ordinary record keeping.

Your choices

  • You can browse the site, docs, and macro library without signing in. An account is needed to start the 6 month trial.
  • You can ask us to delete your account or support emails, unless we need to keep them for a legal or security reason.

Children

SwMacroFlow is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we will update the date at the top.
