Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy applies to SwMacroFlow licences purchased through this website. It should be read alongside our Terms of Use.

The short version

SwMacroFlow payments are non-refundable. We offer a free 1 month trial precisely so that you can evaluate the software on your own files and your own SOLIDWORKS installation, before paying anything at all.

There is nothing to cancel. SwMacroFlow is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. We hold no payment mandate and cannot charge you again.

Try before you pay

  • Every account starts with a free 1 month trial. No card is required to start it.
  • The trial covers macro chaining, batch runs across folders, and the bundled macro library. AI Copilot and Task Scheduler are full version features and are not part of the trial.
  • The trial is the evaluation period. We recommend running SwMacroFlow against your real macros and folders during it.
  • If SwMacroFlow does not suit your workflow, simply do not buy it. Nothing is charged.

Refunds

  • Licence purchases are non-refundable.
  • Changing your mind, no longer needing the software, or not using it after purchase are not grounds for a refund.
  • Because the licence is permanent, you keep what you bought indefinitely, including every future update.

When we will refund

Despite the above, we will refund you where the fault is ours or where the law requires it:

  • Duplicate or incorrect charge. If you were billed twice for the same licence, or charged in error, we refund the incorrect amount in full.
  • Failed access we cannot fix. If a defect on our side prevents you from using SwMacroFlow for a sustained period after you have paid and we cannot resolve it, contact us and we will make it right.
  • Any refund we do issue is returned to the original payment method.

Cancellation

  • There is nothing to cancel. SwMacroFlow is bought once, not subscribed to. We hold no mandate against your card or bank account.
  • Your licence and the application continue working permanently, and updates keep arriving at no extra cost.

Pricing changes

We may change our prices. A price change never affects a licence you have already bought, since your licence is permanent and there is nothing recurring we could reprice.

AI Copilot costs

AI Copilot is bring your own key. The AI provider you choose bills you directly for your usage, on their terms. We do not sell, resell, or refund AI usage, and any refund question about it is between you and that provider.

Contact

For any billing question, email info@swmacroflow.in. See our contact page for full details.
