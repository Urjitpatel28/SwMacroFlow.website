Contact Us

Last updated 13 August 2026

SwMacroFlow is built and supported directly by its developer. Email is the way to reach us, and every message is read by a person. We do not operate a phone line.

Response times

We aim to reply to support and billing email within two working days. SwMacroFlow is a small product, so please include your account email, the SwMacroFlow version, and your SOLIDWORKS version when reporting a problem. It saves a round trip.

Before you write

  • The documentation covers installation, connecting to SOLIDWORKS, building macro chains, and scheduling batches.
  • The macro library lists the bundled macros and what each one does.
  • Billing terms are set out in our refund and cancellation policy.

What we do not support

We can help with SwMacroFlow itself. We do not write or debug your own VBA macros, and we cannot support your SOLIDWORKS installation or licence, which come from Dassault Systemes or your reseller.
