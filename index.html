Features

One file opens. Every selected macro runs. The batch moves on.

SwMacroFlow keeps the workflow obvious: choose files, choose macros, review the run, then let SOLIDWORKS do the repetitive work.

01

Chain multiple macros

Queue as many macros as you need, in the order they should run. Each selected file opens once, every ticked macro runs against it, then the file closes.

02

Batch across folders

Browse a folder recursively, drag and drop, or add files one by one. Parts, assemblies, and drawings are sorted automatically.

03

Standalone application

SwMacroFlow runs beside SOLIDWORKS and drives any installed, licensed x64 version. It joins a session you already have open, or starts one, without registering anything inside SOLIDWORKS.

04

Bundled macro library

Use ready-to-run macros for common jobs such as export and cleanup, while keeping your own VBA macros beside them.

05

AI Copilot side panel Full version

Ask Copilot to build a macro chain, scan a folder, or write a new macro. Destructive actions require your approval first. Bring your own API key — we do not supply one.

06

Task Scheduler batches Full version

Save a setup and run it later through Windows Task Scheduler: once, daily, weekly, monthly, or every N minutes.

FAQ

Good to know before you start.

Which SOLIDWORKS versions does this support?

SwMacroFlow is a standalone Windows application that works with any installed and licensed x64 SOLIDWORKS version. It drives the SOLIDWORKS session on your machine from outside, so nothing is registered or installed inside SOLIDWORKS itself.

What is the difference between the trial and the full version?

The free 1 month trial gives you macro chaining, batch runs across folders, and the bundled macro library. AI Copilot and Task Scheduler are in the full version only. When you buy a licence you get both from day one.

How do I get SwMacroFlow?

Create a SwMacroFlow account to start the free 1 month trial, then download the Windows installer from your account page. No card is required to start. The same account holds your licence and your activated computer.

What does it cost after the trial?

₹2,499, paid once. There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically. The licence is permanent and every future update is included.

Do I get updates after I buy?

Yes. Every future version is included with your licence at no extra cost, and new builds arrive through the app's built-in auto-updater.

Do you provide the AI API key?

No. AI Copilot is bring your own key. You supply a key from the AI provider you choose, and that provider bills you directly for what you use. Your key is stored on your own machine and never reaches us.

Can I schedule batches?

Yes, in the full version. SwMacroFlow can register saved setups with Windows Task Scheduler for one-time, daily, weekly, monthly, or every-N-minute runs.

Can I install it on more than one computer?

You can install SwMacroFlow anywhere, but one licence activates one computer at a time. If you change machines or your PC is reimaged, move the activation from your account page. You can do this yourself, up to twice a month.

Do I need administrator rights?

No. SwMacroFlow uses a per-user installation and is designed to run without an administrator elevation prompt.
